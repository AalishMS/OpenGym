OpenGym — Color System Study

Every color here is solved, not picked: an accent is a seed (hue + chroma), and each role's lightness is bisected in OKLab until it meets a contrast target against its actual ground. Hue and chroma survive; only lightness moves, and only as far as legibility demands. This is the formula that will ship in Dart, so these numbers are the real ones. AA wants 4.5:1 for text and 3:1 for icons and UI edges. Change the accent to confirm every accent holds up — that's the whole point.
